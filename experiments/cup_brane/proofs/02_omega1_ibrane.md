## 0. Results

All statements are for every `n ≥ 1` (for `n = 1` the two red strands both have colour 1).
`R` = Laurent ring `ℤ[t^{±1}, u^{±1}, r^{±1}]` of `GLUING.md`; `R_pol` = polynomial ring.

* **Theorem A (§5.5).** The formula of `GLUING.md` §2 — vertices, terms, coefficients and the
  exterior-algebra signs exactly as stated there — satisfies `d² = 0` over `R`, for every `n`.
  The same holds over `R_pol` after rescaling the envelope `S_k` by the unit monomial
  `f(k) = ∏_{y≤k} π_y` (Cor. 6.5), which makes all coefficients polynomial (minimal such
  rescaling for `n ≥ 2`).
* **Structure of the proof.** (1) all colours are distinct, so a product of two reduced diagrams
  is the reduced diagram times the bigon polynomials of the pairs crossing twice (Lemma 2.3);
  only the `n+1` "edges" `W₁–1, 1–2, …, n–W_n` have non-trivial bigons `Q_0,…,Q_n`.
  (2) A cocycle trick turns the complex into a complex of free modules over the commutative ring
  `S = R[x_1..x_n]` (Prop. 3.2). (3) A change of basis (re-encoding each vertex by the set
  `R = A ∪ C` and rescaling by monomials) turns it into a short Clifford-algebra expression,
  the *R-model* (Def. 4.1, Prop. 4.2), with entries `±1` or `± q_x`, `q_x = T_x Q_x`.
  (4) In the R-model with **free** variables `q_0..q_n` one has the identity
  `δ² = W · H`, `W = Σ_x (−1)^x q_x` (Theorem 5.1), proved by a finite list of Clifford
  computations valid for every block length; (5) `Σ_x (−1)^x T_x Q_x = 0` (Lemma 5.2) is the
  unique linear relation between the `n+1` bigon polynomials — this is where the `τ_x` come from.
  No case list over pairs of terms is needed; the proof's own case scheme (labels of §5.6) was
  checked against **all** composable pairs for `n ≤ 8` (`02_cases.py`).
* **Theorem B (§6).** Every term is homogeneous for the full multigrading, with the explicit
  vertex degree `Δ(v) = Σ_x m_x(v)(q + ε_x)`, `m_x = o_x + 2[x < k] + 2[x ∈ C]`; within each
  envelope `Δ` is the cube grading of `PROOFS.md` up to a constant, and the envelope shifts are
  those of `standard_ebranes`. Every coefficient is the unique Laurent monomial of its degree.
* **Theorem C (§7, Thm. 7.3).** In the degree of the differential there are no
  homotopies between different envelopes and the only homogeneous elements between different
  envelopes are the formula's dot-free terms. Consequently, for every `n` and `k`, the space of
  degree-0 maps `S_k → C_{k−1}` (`C_{k−1}` = the formula on `S_0..S_{k−1}`) in the homotopy
  category is free of rank one, generated by the formula's gluing map. **Hence the formula is the
  iterated cone over the unique Ext classes, for every `n`.** Independently of degrees, every
  first-order component `S_k → S_{k−1}` is not null-homotopic (Prop. 7.4).
* **Theorem D (§8, Thm. 8.1).** Over `R` the I-brane is a projective resolution of
  the module `L` that is `R` (dots acting by 0) at the single idempotent `W₁ 1 2 … n W_n` and 0 at
  every other idempotent.
* Errors / corrections to `GLUING.md`: none in §2 (the formula, signs and coefficients are
  confirmed). Two small remarks in §10.
* Only the relations of the algebra (two red strands) are used. The basis theorem of
  `01_foundations.md` (proved there for one red strand) is not needed: for distinct labels its
  content — `e(s)Ae(s')` is free of rank one over the dots, with basis the reduced diagram — is
  proved directly (Lemma 2.5) from the relations and the polynomial modules `Pol_b` (Remark 3.4).

## 1. Setting

### 1.1 The algebra

**Definition 1.1.** Fix `n ≥ 1`. *Labels*: `0 := W₁` (red, colour 1), `1, …, n` (black, colour
`c` for label `c`), `n+1 := W_n` (red, colour `n`). A *state* is a sequence listing every label
once, with `0` before `n+1`. The algebra `A = A_n` (over `R_pol` or `R`) is the presentation of
`proofs/01_foundations.md` Definition A.1 extended in the evident way to **two** red strands:
generators `e(s)`, dots `x_{p,s}` on black positions, crossings `ψ_{i,s}` for every `i` such that
positions `i, i+1` of `s` are not both red (there is no red–red crossing); relations (A0)–(A6)
of Def. A.1, where in (A5) a black strand of colour 1 and `W₁` give `u_1 x`, a black strand of
colour `n` and `W_n` give `u_n x`, every other black–red pair gives `1`, and in (A6) the red
exception is "two black strands of the colour of the red middle strand". (For `n = 1` both red
strands have colour 1 and the one black strand gives `u_1x` with each.) This is what `klrw`
implements: bigons and braid relations were compared with `klrw` on every state for `n ≤ 5`
(`02_lemmaA.py`, *sage*). The grading is (F2) with two symbols `ε_{1W}, ε_{nW}` (one if `n = 1`).

We use **only these relations**; the basis theorem `01_foundations.md` A.5 (proved there for one
red strand) is never invoked — for distinct labels its content is elementary (Lemma 2.5).

The only relations that can occur here, all colours being distinct:

* (R1) far-apart generators commute, dots commute;
* (R2) a dot slides through every crossing (both strands of a crossing have different labels);
* (R3) `ψ_i² e(s) = Q e(s)` where `Q` depends on the two labels `a, b` at positions `i,i+1`:
  `Q = t_{ab}x_a + t_{ba}x_b` if `a, b` are black with `|a − b| = 1`; `Q = u_1 x_1` for
  `{a,b} = {0,1}`; `Q = u_n x_n` for `{a,b} = {n, n+1}`; `Q = 1` otherwise (either order);
* (R4) `ψ_iψ_{i+1}ψ_i = ψ_{i+1}ψ_iψ_{i+1}` on every state on which both sides are defined (Lemma 2.1).

**Edges.** For `0 ≤ x ≤ n` the *edge* `x` is the pair of labels `{x, x+1}`. Put `x_0 = x_{n+1} := 0`,
`t_{1,0} := u_1`, `t_{n,n+1} := u_n` and

    Q_x := t_{x,x+1} x_x + t_{x+1,x} x_{x+1}       (0 ≤ x ≤ n),

so `Q_0 = u_1x_1`, `Q_n = u_n x_n`, and by (R3) the bigon of a pair of labels is `Q_x` if the pair is
edge `x`, and `1` otherwise. (For `n = 1`: `Q_0 = Q_1 = u_1 x_1`.) Checked against `klrw` on every
state for `n ≤ 5`: `02_lemmaA.py` (*sage*, "local relations").

### 1.2 The formula

**Definition 1.2 (GLUING.md §2).** *Vertices* `v = (k; A, C)`, `0 ≤ k ≤ n`, `A ⊆ [1,k]`,
`C ⊆ [k+1,n]`, `B = [1,n] ∖ (A ∪ C)`; each colour `c` has a *state* `A`, `B_L` (`c ≤ k, c ∈ B`),
`B_R` (`c > k, c ∈ B`) or `C`. Idempotent `e_v = [A decreasing] W₁ [B increasing] W_n [C decreasing]`;
homological degree `hdeg(v) = k + |B_L| + |C|` `= Σ_c h(state of c)` with `h(B_L)=2`,
`h(A)=h(C)=1`, `h(B_R)=0`. *Terms* out of `v`, `l = k − d`, *block* `[l+1, k]`:

| type | condition on `v` | target `w` | coefficient |
|---|---|---|---|
| int L | `c ≤ k`, `c ∈ B_L` | `c → A` | 1 |
| int R | `c > k`, `c ∈ C` | `c → B_R` | 1 |
| (i)_{l,k} | block `= A^d` | `(l; …)`, block `→ C^{d−1}B_R` | 1 |
| (ii)_{l,k} | block `= B_L A^{d−1}` | block `→ C^d` | `∏_{x=l+1}^k τ_x` |
| (iii)_{l,k,c}, `c ≤ l` | block `= B_L A^{d−1}`, `c ∈ A` | block `→ C^{d−1}B_R`, `c → B_L` | `∏_{x=c}^{l} τ_x^{−1}` |
| (iii)_{l,k,c}, `c > k` | block `= B_L A^{d−1}`, `c ∈ B_R` | block `→ C^{d−1}B_R`, `c → C` | `∏_{x=l+1}^{c} τ_x` |

(`d = 1`: (iii) block `B_L → B_R`.) `τ_x = t_{x,x−1}/t_{x,x+1}`. The diagram of every term is the
reduced diagram `β(e_v, e_w)` (Lemma 2.2). Signs: the exterior-algebra rule of `GLUING.md` §2
(generators `a_1<…<a_n<g_n<…<g_1`; `B_R ↔ 1`, `A ↔ a_c`, `C ↔ g_c`, `B_L ↔ a_cg_c`; operators
`ι(g_c)`; `(−1)^{d+1}ι(a_k)∏ρ`; `∏ρ ι(a_{l+1})`; `± ε(g_c)ι(a_k)∏ρ ι(a_{l+1})`;
`± ε(g_c)ι(g_k)ι(a_k)` with `± = (−1)^{l+1−c+d}`, resp. `(−1)^{c−k}`). The differential is
`d e_v = Σ_terms sign · coefficient · β(e_v, e_w)` (right multiplication, `P_v → P_w`).

`02_model.py` implements this from the text (not from `glue/`) and agrees with
`glue/omega1_rule.py` + `glue/omega1_formula.py` term by term, sign by sign, for `n ≤ 7`.
Vertices of one envelope `S_k` are `2^n`; all vertices `(n+1)2^n`.

### 1.3 Order bits

**Definition 1.3.** For a state `s` and an edge `x`, `o_x(s) = 1` if label `x` is to the left of
label `x+1` in `s`, else `0`. For a vertex, `o_x(v) := o_x(e_v)`; put `α_c(v) = [c ∉ A]`,
`γ_c(v) = [c ∉ C]` for `1 ≤ c ≤ n`, and `γ_0 = α_{n+1} = 1`.

**Lemma 1.4.** `o_x(v) = α_{x+1}(v) · γ_x(v)` for `0 ≤ x ≤ n`.

*Proof.* Positions in `e_v`: label `c ∈ A` at `−c`, `c ∈ B` at `c`, `c ∈ C` at `2n+2−c`, `W₁` at
`0`, `W_n` at `n+1` (this order is exactly `e_v`: `A` decreasing, then `W₁`, `B` increasing,
`W_n`, `C` decreasing). Treat `W₁` and `W_n` as labels in region `B`. For labels `y < y'` in regions
`X, X'` ∈ {A < B < C}: if `X ≠ X'` then `y` is left of `y'` iff `X < X'`; if `X = X' = B` then yes; if
`X = X' ∈ {A, C}` then no. Hence `y` is left of `y'` iff `X' ≠ A` and `X ≠ C` (check the nine
cases). For `(y, y') = (x, x+1)` this is `α_{x+1}γ_x`. ∎

## 2. Products of reduced diagrams

**Lemma 2.1 (no bad triples).** Every braid relation `ψ_iψ_{i+1}ψ_i = ψ_{i+1}ψ_iψ_{i+1}` that can
be applied in `A_n` holds exactly.

*Proof.* A braid move involves three strands that cross pairwise. The two red strands never cross,
so at most one of the three is red; the other two (or three) are black with pairwise different
labels, hence pairwise different colours. The two bad cases of (R4) need two **black** strands of
equal colour (bad-1: outer strands black of colour `c`; bad-2: outer strands black of colour `k`,
the red one in the middle). Neither occurs. Case `n = 1`: both red strands have colour 1 but still
never cross, and there is one black strand. ∎
Checked on every state for `n ≤ 5` (`02_lemmaA.py`, *sage*: 10 800 braid relations for `n = 5`).

**Lemma 2.2 (β is well defined).** For states `s, s'` there is exactly one bijection of strands
`s → s'` (labels are distinct); all reduced words of it give the same element `β(s, s') ∈ e(s)Ae(s')`.
A pair of labels crosses in `β(s,s')` iff its relative order differs in `s` and `s'`.

*Proof.* Matsumoto (F3): reduced words are connected by commutation moves (R1) and braid moves,
which are equalities by Lemma 2.1. The last sentence is the usual description of the inversion set. ∎
(Also: `proofs/01_foundations.md` proves well-definedness of term diagrams in general; here the
elementary argument suffices. Checked: a second reduced word gives the same element in all
tested cases, `02_lemmaA.py`.)

**Lemma 2.3 (product rule).** For states `s, s', s''`,

    β(s, s') · β(s', s'') = Q_{D(s,s',s'')} · β(s, s''),       Q_D := ∏_{x ∈ D} Q_x,

where `D(s,s',s'')` is the set of edges `x` with `o_x(s') ≠ o_x(s) = o_x(s'')` (the edges whose two
labels cross twice), and the polynomial `Q_D` in the dots may be written at either end (R2).

*Proof.* Induction on `ℓ(s',s'')` = number of pairs inverted between `s'` and `s''`. If it is 0,
`s' = s''`. Otherwise take a reduced word of `β(s',s'')` beginning with `ψ_i` (on `s'`), so
`β(s',s'') = ψ_i β(s'_1, s'')` with `s'_1 = s'` with positions `i, i+1` exchanged and
`ℓ(s'_1, s'') = ℓ − 1`. Let `a, b` be the labels at positions `i, i+1` of `s'`.
*Case 1:* `a, b` are in the same order in `s` and `s'`. The inversion set of `(s, s'_1)` is that of
`(s, s')` plus `{a,b}`, so (reduced word of `β(s,s')`)·`ψ_i` is a reduced word and
`β(s,s')ψ_i = β(s, s'_1)` (Lemma 2.2).
*Case 2:* the order differs. Then `ℓ(s,s'_1) = ℓ(s,s') − 1` and (reduced word of
`β(s,s'_1)`)·`ψ_i` is a reduced word of `(s,s')`, so `β(s,s') = β(s,s'_1)ψ_i` and
`β(s,s')ψ_i = β(s,s'_1)ψ_i² = β(s, s'_1) Q_{ab}` by (R3), `Q_{ab}` the bigon of `{a,b}` (`Q_x` if
`{a,b}` is edge `x`, else 1); by (R2) `Q_{ab}` commutes with every diagram.
In both cases apply the induction hypothesis to `β(s,s'_1)β(s'_1,s'')`. Comparing the sets: pairs
other than `{a,b}` have the same order in `s'` and `s'_1`; `{a,b}` has in `s'_1` the order of `s''`,
so it is not in `D(s,s'_1,s'')`, and it is in `D(s,s',s'')` iff Case 2. Non-edge pairs contribute 1. ∎

*Check* (`02_lemmaA.py`, *sage*, `klrw`, polynomial parameters): all 27 / 1728 triples for `n = 1, 2`,
random 2000 / 3000 / 1500 triples for `n = 3, 4, 5`: 0 mismatches.
(The same statement is `proofs/05_main_sliding.md` Lemma 1.3 in a more general setting; the
proof above uses only (R1)–(R4) and holds verbatim with two red strands.)

**Lemma 2.5 (structure of `A_n`; replaces the basis theorem).** Let `S := R[x_1..x_n]`, the dot
of label `c` written `x_c` (at any position: (R2)). For all states `s, s'`, `e(s) A e(s')` is a
free `S`-module of rank one with basis `β(s, s')`; multiplication is given by Lemma 2.3.

*Proof.* *Spanning:* an element is a combination of products of generators; dots move to the
left through crossings ((A3), (A4) with distinct labels, exact), and a word `ψ_{i_1}⋯ψ_{i_r}`
from `s` equals `Q·β(s, s_r)` by induction on `r`, applying Lemma 2.3 to
`β(s, s_{r−1}) · β(s_{r−1}, s_r)` (`β` of one crossing is that crossing). *Independence:* the
module `Pol_b` of Remark 3.4 is an `A`-module, and `v_s · f β(s,s') = f Q_{D_b(s,s')} v_{s'}`;
since `S` is a domain and `Q_{D_b} ≠ 0`, `fβ(s,s') = 0` forces `f = 0`. ∎

(`proofs/05_main_sliding.md` Lemmas 1.2/1.3 contain Lemma 2.3 for general words; their
hypotheses (S1), (S2) and "no strand of an adjacent pair crosses a strand of its own colour" hold
trivially here because all black colours are distinct and red strands do not cross; their proof
uses only relations, so it applies verbatim to two red strands. Our Lemma 2.3 is self-contained.)

**Corollary 2.4.** For terms `v → u → w` with coefficients `c_{vu}, c_{uw}` (signs included)
the product is `c_{vu}c_{uw} Q_{D(e_v,e_u,e_w)} β(e_v,e_w)`. Hence `d² = 0` follows from

    (Z_{v,w})     Σ_u c_{vu} c_{uw} Q_{D(v,u,w)} = 0 ∈ S := R[x_1, …, x_n]   for all v, w.

(Sufficiency needs no basis theorem; the converse would use it.) This is where all relations are
used; from now on everything happens in the commutative ring `S`.

## 3. The scalar complex

**Lemma 3.1 (cocycle).** For any `b ∈ {0,1}^{n+1}` put `D_b(v,u) := {x : o_x(u) = b_x ≠ o_x(v)}`.
Then, as multisets, `D_b(v,u) + D_b(u,w) = D(v,u,w) + D_b(v,w)`.

*Proof.* Edge by edge; with `β = b_x` and the bits `o_x` of `v, u, w`, normalise `β = 0`; the eight
cases `(o(v),o(u),o(w)) ∈ {0,1}³` give `[0,0]→0=0`, `001: 0=0`, `010: 1=1`, `011: 0=0`,
`100: 1=1`, `101: 1=1`, `110: 1=1`, `111: 0=0`. ∎

**Proposition 3.2 (scalar reduction).** Fix `b`. Let `K_b` be the complex of free `S`-modules with
basis `{e_v}` and differential `δ e_v = Σ_{terms v→w} c_{vw} Q_{D_b(v,w)} e_w`. If `δ² = 0` then
the I-brane satisfies `d² = 0`.

*Proof.* `δ²` has `(v,w)` entry `Σ_u c_{vu}c_{uw}Q_{D_b(v,u)}Q_{D_b(u,w)} = Q_{D_b(v,w)} Σ_u c_{vu}c_{uw}Q_{D(v,u,w)}`
(Lemma 3.1). `S` is a domain and `Q_{D_b(v,w)} ≠ 0`, so `(Z_{v,w})` holds. ∎

**Remark 3.4 (the modules `Pol_b`).** For `b ∈ {0,1}^{n+1}` let `Pol_b = ⊕_s S v_s` (all states),
with `v_s e(s') = δ_{ss'}v_s`, `v_s x_{p} = x_{(label at p)} v_s`, and `v_s ψ_i = Q_x v_{s_i}` if the
two labels exchanged form edge `x` and `o_x(s_i) = b_x`, `v_s ψ_i = v_{s_i}` otherwise.
*This is a right `A`-module:* (A0), (A1) clear; (A2), (A3), (A4) (distinct labels): the factors
depend only on the pair exchanged and its new order, and dots act by multiplication;
(A5): of the two crossings of `ψ_i²` exactly one ends with the pair in order `b_x`, giving
`Q_x`, resp. `1` for a non-edge — this is (A5); (A6): `C = 0` (Lemma 2.1) and both sides are
reduced words of the same permutation of three strands, so the same pairs end in order `b`.
Hence `v_s β(s,s') = Q_{D_b(s,s')} v_{s'}` and `K_b = Pol_b ⊗_A E` (for a complex `E` of projectives
`P_v = Ae_v`, `Pol_b ⊗_A Ae_v = S v_{e_v}`). For `b = o(s₀)`, `s₀` a state, `Pol_b ≅ e(s₀)A`
(Lemma 2.5), so `K_{o(s₀)} = e(s₀)·E = Hom_A(Ae(s₀), E)`. Used in Lemma 2.5, Prop. 7.4, §8.

From now on `b = (1, …, 1)` (the order bits of the state `W₁ 1 2 … n W_n`).

**Lemma 3.3 (bigon factors for b = 1).** The factor `Q_{D_1(v,w)}` of each term is:
int L: 1; int R at `c`: `Q_c`; (i)_{l,k}: `Q_l`; (ii): 1; (iii) with `c ≤ l`: `Q_{c−1}`; (iii) with `c > k`: 1.

*Proof.* `D_1(v,w)` = edges with `o_x: 0 → 1`. Use Lemma 1.4. int L (`c: B_L→A`): `α_c: 1→0`, so
`o_{c−1}: 1→0` (`γ_{c−1}=1`) and `o_c = α_{c+1}γ_c` unchanged. int R (`c: C→B_R`): `γ_c: 0→1`, `α_{c+1} = 1`
(`c+1` is R-type or `n+1`): `o_c: 0→1`; `o_{c−1} = α_cγ_{c−1}` unchanged. (i): `o_l = α_{l+1}γ_l: 0→1`;
for `l < x < k`: `o_x = α_{x+1}γ_x` is 0 before (`x+1 ∈ A`) and after (`x ∈ C`); `o_k`: `γ_k = 1` and
`α_{k+1}` unchanged. (ii): `o_l` stays `1·1`; inner edges `0→0`; `o_k: 1→0`. (iii) without the extra
colour: `o_l` stays 1, inner edges `0→0`, `o_k` stays 1 (`k ∈ A → B`); for `d = 1` nothing changes
region. Extra colour `c ≤ l`, `A→B_L`: `α_c: 0→1`, `o_{c−1}: 0→1`; extra `c > k`, `B_R→C`:
`o_c: 1→0`. ∎ (`02_model.py` / exploratory check for `n ≤ 7`.)

## 4. The R-model

**Definition 4.1 (R-model).** Let `q_0, …, q_n` be indeterminates (or elements of any commutative
ring). Re-encode a vertex as `(k, R)` with `R = A ∪ C ⊆ [1,n]` (arbitrary; `A = R ∩ [1,k]`,
`C = R ∖ [1,k]`). Let `Λ = Λ(r_1, …, r_n)`, basis `r_R` (`R` increasing), with `ε_c, ι_c` the left
multiplication / contraction (`ε_c r_R = (−1)^{|R_{<c}|} r_{R∪c}` for `c ∉ R`, etc.),
`P^1_c = ε_cι_c`, `P^0_c = ι_cε_c`, `Π_{[a,b]} = ∏_{c=a}^b P^1_c`. On `V = ⊕_{k=0}^n e_k ⊗ Λ` put
`δ(e_k ⊗ m) = e_k ⊗ ∂_k m + Σ_{l<k} e_l ⊗ Z_{l,k} m`, where, with `d = k − l`,

    ∂_k     = Σ_{c≤k} ε_c + Σ_{c>k} q_c ι_c,
    Z_{l,k} = −q_l ι_k Π_{[l+1,k]} + ( (−1)^{d+1} ε_{l+1} + E_{l,k} ι_k ε_{l+1} ) Π_{[l+2,k]},
    E_{l,k} = Σ_{c≤l} (−1)^{l−c} q_{c−1} ι_c − Σ_{c>k} (−1)^{c−l} ε_c .

The three summands of `Z` are the terms (i), (ii), (iii) (for `d = 1`, `ι_kε_k = P^0_k`). So
`δ² = 0` iff `Φ_{j,k} := Σ_{j≤l≤k} Z_{j,l}Z_{l,k} = 0` for all `j ≤ k` (`Z_{k,k} := ∂_k`; plain
products, no signs).

## 5. d² = 0

**Lemma 5.2 (the linear relation).** `Σ_{x=0}^n (−1)^x T_x Q_x = 0`. Conversely, if
`a ∈ R^{n+1}` and `Σ a_x T_x Q_x = 0` then `a = a_0 ((−1)^x)_x`.

*Proof.* Coefficient of `x_c` (`1 ≤ c ≤ n`) in `Σ a_xT_xQ_x`: `a_{c−1}T_{c−1}t_{c,c−1} + a_cT_ct_{c,c+1}`;
since `T_c = T_{c−1}t_{c,c−1}/t_{c,c+1}` this is `T_{c−1}t_{c,c−1}(a_{c−1} + a_c)`; it vanishes for all
`c` iff `a_c = −a_{c−1}`. ∎ (Polynomial form, multiplying by `π_1` of §6: `Σ_x (−1)^x π_{x+1} Q_x = 0`.)

**Theorem 5.1 (universal identity).** In the R-model over `ℤ[q_0, …, q_n]`, for `j < k`,

    Φ_{j,k} = (−1)^{j+1} W N_{j,k},      W = Σ_{x=0}^n (−1)^x q_x,   N_{j,k} = ι_k ε_{j+1} Π_{[j+2,k]},

and `Φ_{k,k} = ∂_k² = 0`. So `δ² = W·H`, `H = Σ_{j<k} (−1)^{j+1} e_j e_k^* ⊗ N_{j,k}`.

*Proof.* Clifford rules: `ε_cε_{c'} = −ε_{c'}ε_c`, `ι_cι_{c'} = −ι_{c'}ι_c`, `ε_cι_{c'}+ι_{c'}ε_c = δ_{cc'}`;
`P` even, commuting with all operators of other colours; `ι_cP^1_c = ι_c`, `P^1_cι_c = 0`,
`ε_cP^1_c = 0`, `P^1_cε_c = ε_c`, `ε_cP^0_c = ε_c`, `P^0_cε_c = 0`, `ι_cP^0_c = 0`, `P^0_cι_c = ι_c`.
Identities are identities in the Clifford algebra, so they may be verified with any order of
generators. `∂_k² = 0`: the `ε_c` (`c ≤ k`) and `ι_c` (`c > k`) have distinct colours and anticommute.

*Case D := k − j = 1.* `Z = −q_{k−1}ι_k + ε_k + E P^0_k`, `E = E_{k−1,k}`, `∂_k = ∂' + ε_k`,
`∂_{k−1} = ∂' + q_kι_k` with `∂' = Σ_{c<k}ε_c + Σ_{c>k}q_cι_c`. `∂'` anticommutes with the colour-`k`
part, so `∂'`-terms give `[∂', E]_+ P^0_k = (Σ_{c<k}(−1)^{k−1−c}q_{c−1} + Σ_{c>k}(−1)^{c−k}q_c)P^0_k
= ((−1)^kW + q_{k−1} − q_k)P^0_k`. Colour `k`: `q_kι_k(−q_{k−1}ι_k + ε_k + EP^0_k) + (−q_{k−1}ι_k + ε_k + EP^0_k)ε_k
= q_kP^0_k − q_{k−1}P^0_k`. Total `(−1)^kWP^0_k = (−1)^{j+1}WN_{k−1,k}`.

*Case D ≥ 2: reduction to the block.* *Inner* colours `(j,k]`, *outer* colours the others.
`∂_j = ∂^{out} + Σ_{c∈(j,k]} q_cι_c`, `∂_k = ∂^{out} + Σ_{c∈(j,k]} ε_c`,
`∂^{out} = Σ_{c≤j}ε_c + Σ_{c>k}q_cι_c`. For `j ≤ a < b ≤ k`:
`E_{a,b} = (−1)^a(O + I_{a,b})` with `O = Σ_{c≤j}(−1)^cq_{c−1}ι_c − Σ_{c>k}(−1)^cε_c` (outer, the same for
all `a, b`) and `I_{a,b} = Σ_{c∈(j,a]}(−1)^cq_{c−1}ι_c − Σ_{c∈(b,k]}(−1)^cε_c` (inner). So
`Z_{a,b} = Z^{in}_{a,b} + (−1)^a O N_{a,b}` with `Z^{in}` inner and odd, `N` inner and even, `I_{j,k} = 0`.
Expanding `Φ_{j,k} = ∂_jZ_{j,k} + Z_{j,k}∂_k + Σ_{j<l<k}Z_{j,l}Z_{l,k}`:

* `∂^{out}`-terms: `∂^{out}Z_{j,k} + Z_{j,k}∂^{out} = (−1)^j[∂^{out}, O]_+ N_{j,k} = (−1)^j(−W + W_{[j,k]})N_{j,k}`,
  `W_{[j,k]} := Σ_{x=j}^k (−1)^x q_x`. (*Labels* `dO` — two orders of an outer internal move and a
  gluing term, cancel — and `Wsat` — the outer internal move undoes the extra colour of (iii).)
* terms with two `O`'s: `(−1)^{j+l}ON_{j,l}ON_{l,k} = (−1)^{j+l}O²N_{j,l}N_{l,k} = 0` (`O² = 0`).
  (*Label* `OO`: the two paths with the two extra colours exchanged cancel.)
* terms linear in `O`: `(−1)^j O Ψ_{j,k}`, with
  `Ψ_{j,k} = −∂^{in}_jN_{j,k} + N_{j,k}∂^{in}_k + Σ_{j<l<k}[N_{j,l}Z^{in}_{l,k} − (−1)^{l−j}Z^{in}_{j,l}N_{l,k}]`.
* terms without `O`: `Φ^{in}_{j,k} = ∂^{in}_jZ^{in}_{j,k} + Z^{in}_{j,k}∂^{in}_k + Σ_{j<l<k}Z^{in}_{j,l}Z^{in}_{l,k}`.

Shifting colours by `−j` (`c' = c − j`, `q'_{x'} = q_{x'+j}`) leaves the formulas for `A, B, (−1)^aI, ∂^{in}`
unchanged (all signs are `(−1)^{a+c}` or `(−1)^{b−a}`) and turns `W_{[j,k]}` into `(−1)^jW'_D`,
`W'_D = Σ_{x=0}^D (−1)^x q'_x`. So it suffices to show, for the block `[1,D]`, `j = 0`, `k = D ≥ 2`:
**Lemma 5.3** `Ψ = 0` and **Lemma 5.4** `Φ^{in} = −W'_D N_{0,D}`. Then
`Φ_{j,k} = (−1)^j(−W + W_{[j,k]})N − (−1)^jW_{[j,k]}N = (−1)^{j+1}WN_{j,k}`. ∎

Notation for the block: `A_{a,b} = −q_aι_bΠ_{[a+1,b]}`, `B_{a,b} = (−1)^{b−a+1}ε_{a+1}Π_{[a+2,b]}`,
`C_{a,b} = (−1)^aI_{a,b}N_{a,b}`, `Z^{in} = A + B + C`; `I_{l,D} = Σ_{c≤l}(−1)^cq_{c−1}ι_c`,

## 6. Degrees (Theorem B)

Grading (F2), with the two red strands: write `E_x := q + ε_x` for the degree of a crossing of the
two labels of edge `x` (`ε_0 = ε_{1W}`, `ε_x = ε_{x,x+1}`, `ε_n = ε_{nW}`; for `n = 1`, `ε_0 = ε_1`).
Convention of `PROOFS.md` §1.3: `P_v` is shifted by `−Δ(v)`, and a component `P_v → P_w` is right
multiplication by an element of degree `Δ(v) − Δ(w)`.

**Proposition 6.1.** `deg β(s,s') = Σ_{x : o_x(s) ≠ o_x(s')} E_x`.

*Proof.* Each inverted pair crosses once (Lemma 2.2); by (F2) a crossing has degree `E_x` if its two
strands form edge `x` and `0` otherwise (distinct non-adjacent colours, or a black strand and a
red strand of another colour). ∎ Checked against `klrw`'s degree, 400 random pairs each for
`n ≤ 5` (`02_lemmaA.py`).

**Definition 6.2.** `Δ(v) := Σ_{x=0}^n m_x(v) E_x`,  `m_x(v) := o_x(v) + 2·[x ≤ k−1] + 2·[x ∈ C]`
(`v = (k;A,C)`; `[x ∈ C]` for the colour `x`).

**Theorem 6.3 (homogeneity).** Every term `t: v → w` with coefficient `μ_t` (Laurent monomial) is
homogeneous: `deg μ_t + deg β(e_v,e_w) = Δ(v) − Δ(w)`.

*Proof.* `deg t_{x,x−1} = 2ε_{x−1} − δ_x`, `deg t_{x,x+1} = 2ε_x − δ_x`, so `deg τ_x = 2E_{x−1} − 2E_x`.
By Prop. 6.1 and Def. 6.2 the `E_x`-coefficient of `Δ(v) − Δ(w) − deg β` is
`−2[x ∈ F] + 2[l ≤ x ≤ k−1] + 2([x ∈ C_v] − [x ∈ C_w])`, `F` = edges with `o_x: 0 → 1` (as listed in
Lemma 3.3). Per type: int L (`F = ∅`, `l = k`, `C` fixed): 0. int R (`F = {c}`, `C` loses `c`): 0.
(i) (`F = {l}`, `C` gains `[l+1,k−1]`): 0. (ii) (`F = ∅`, `C` gains `[l+1,k]`): `2E_l − 2E_k = deg ∏_{l+1}^kτ_x`.
(iii), `c ≤ l` (`F = {c−1}`, `C` gains `[l+1,k−1]`): `−2E_{c−1} + 2E_l = deg ∏_{x=c}^lτ_x^{−1}`.
(iii), `c > k` (`F = ∅`, `C` gains `[l+1,k−1] ∪ {c}`): `2E_l − 2E_c = deg ∏_{l+1}^cτ_x`. ∎

## 7. Identification with the iterated cone (Theorem C)

### 7.1 The envelopes

`S_k` = the vertices `(k; A, C)` with the internal terms. Its idempotents are those of the product of
two cubes: stack 1 (red `W₁`, word `1, 2, …, k`, vertex `I_1 = B_L`), stack 2 (red `W_n`, colours
`k+1, …, n`, vertex `I_2 = C`; the order `[B_R increasing] W_n [C decreasing]` is that of
`state_by_index` in `klrw/stable_envelopes.py`). Its terms are the elementary moves of the two
stacks with coefficient 1. By `PROOFS.md` Thm. 5 + Prop. 6 the product of the two uncorrected
cubes is a complex, and so is `S_k` (Theorem A). Two sign systems on the same cube that both make
every square anticommute differ by a vertex sign (the sign ratio is a 1-cocycle on the cube's
2-skeleton, whose `H¹(−;ℤ/2)` vanishes), so `S_k` **is** the stable envelope up to isomorphism,
with the grading shifts of Cor. 6.4(a),(b).

### 7.2 The degree principle

For `v ∈ S_k`, `w ∈ S_l`, `l < k`, `d = k − l`, let `F(v,w)` = edges with `o_x: 0 → 1` from `v` to `w`.

**Lemma 7.1 (★).** `|A_v| − |A_w| ≤ d − 1 + |F(v,w)|`. If moreover `hdeg(w) = hdeg(v) − 1`, equality
holds iff `(v, w)` is the pair of a term of the formula.

*Proof.* Every `c ∈ A_v ∖ A_w` has `o_{c−1}(v) = 0` (Lemma 1.4) and `o_{c−1}(w) = [c−1 ∉ C_w]`; so
`c−1 ∈ F` unless `c−1 ∈ C_w`, which needs `c ≤ k` and `c − 1 > l`, i.e. `c ∈ [l+2,k]`: at most `d−1`
colours. Hence `|A_v| − |A_w| ≤ |A_v∖A_w| ≤ |F| + d − 1`.
*Equality* forces: (1) `A_w ⊆ A_v`; (2) `[l+2,k] ⊆ A_v` and `[l+1,k−1] ⊆ C_w`; (3) `F` is exactly
`{c−1 : c ∈ A_v∖A_w, c−1 ∉ C_w}`. Groups: `X = [1,l]` (L-type in `v` and `w`), block `Y = [l+1,k]`,
`Z = [k+1,n]` (R-type in both). By (1) no `X`-colour goes `B_L → A`. Computing `o_x` with Lemma
1.4: for `x ≥ k+1`, `o_x = γ_x`, so `x ∈ F` iff `x` goes `C → B_R`, which (3) excludes; edge `k` has
`o_k(v) = 1`; edges in `[l+1,k−1]` are `0` in `v` and `w`; edge `l ∈ F` iff `l+1 ∈ A_v` (consistent
with (3)); edges `x < l`: `x ∈ F` iff `x+1` goes `A → B_L` (consistent with (3)). So (3) ⟺ no
`Z`-colour goes `C → B_R`. Let `a` = #(`X`: `A → B_L`), `z` = #(`Z`: `B_R → C`), `β = [l+1 ∈ B_L(v)]`,
`κ = [k ∈ C_w]`. Then `hdeg(w) − hdeg(v) = −1 + a − β + κ + z`, so `hdeg(w) = hdeg(v) − 1` gives
`a + κ + z = β ≤ 1`. `β = 0`: nothing else moves, block `A^d → C^{d−1}B_R`: (i). `β = 1`: exactly one of
`κ = 1` (block `B_L A^{d−1} → C^d`: (ii)), `a = 1` ((iii), extra `c ≤ l`), `z = 1` ((iii), extra `c > k`).
Conversely every term attains equality. ∎ (`02_degree.py`: all pairs, `n ≤ 7`.)

**Corollary 7.2 (no homotopies; only the formula's terms).** Let `v ∈ S_k`, `w ∈ S_l`, `l < k`,
and let `z ∈ e_v A e_w` be homogeneous of degree `Δ(v) − Δ(w)` (over `R` or `R_pol`).
(H) If `hdeg(w) = hdeg(v)` then `z = 0`. (C) If `hdeg(w) = hdeg(v) − 1` then `z = 0` unless `(v,w)`
is a term `t` of the formula, and then `z ∈ ℤ·μ_t β(e_v, e_w)`.

*Proof.* By Lemma 2.5, `z = fβ`, `f = Σ c_{α,μ} μ x^α`, each summand of degree `Δ(v)−Δ(w) − deg β`.
The `q`-component: `2|α| = Σ_x(m_x(v) − m_x(w)) − #{flipped edges} = 2(d + |C_v| − |C_w| − |F|)`. With
`hdeg = 2k − |A| + |C|` this is `|α| = (hdeg(v) − hdeg(w)) − d + |A_v| − |A_w| − |F| ≤ hdeg(v) − hdeg(w) − 1`
by (★). So `|α| < 0` in case (H); in case (C) `|α| ≤ 0` with equality iff `(v,w)` is a term; then `μ`
is unique by Cor. 6.4(c), equal to `μ_t`, and `c ∈ ℤ` (the degree-0 part of `R`). ∎
(`02_degree.py`: the `q`-count and both statements, all pairs, `n ≤ 7`.)

### 7.3 Uniqueness of the gluing maps

Let `C_{k−1}` be the twisted complex given by the formula on `S_0, …, S_{k−1}` (a complex by
Theorem A). A *gluing datum* is `g = Σ_{l<k} g_{l,k}`, `g_{l,k}: S_k → S_l` homogeneous of degree
`Δ`, lowering `hdeg` by one, such that `δ_g := δ_{C_{k−1}} + ∂_{S_k} + g` squares to zero (`g` is a
chain map `S_k → C_{k−1}` in the twisted-complex convention). Two data are homotopic iff they
differ by `∂h ± h∂` with `h` of the same degree preserving `hdeg`; by Cor. 7.2(H), `h = 0`.

**Theorem 7.3.** For every `n` and `1 ≤ k ≤ n` the gluing data form the group `ℤ · Z_{•,k}`, where
`Z_{•,k}` is the formula's map. Hence `Hom_{K}(S_k, C_{k−1})` in the degree of the differential is
free of rank one, generated by the formula's map, and the formula is the iterated cone
`C_n = Cone(S_n → Cone(S_{n−1} → ⋯ Cone(S_1 → S_0)))` over the unique Ext classes. The same holds in
the polynomial model.

*Proof.* By Cor. 7.2(C), `g = Σ_{t} c_t·t` over the formula's terms `t` out of `S_k`, `c_t ∈ ℤ`. The
components of `δ_g²` are of the form `fβ(e_v,e_w)`; by Lemma 2.5 and Remark 3.4 they vanish iff
their images in `K_1` vanish, so we may compute in the R-model with `q_x = T_xQ_x` (Prop. 4.2),
whose relation module is `ℤ·((−1)^x)` (Lemma 5.2, converse).

*Step 1 (d = 1).* The components from `S_k` to `S_{k−1}` are `∂_{k−1}g_{k−1,k} + g_{k−1,k}∂_k = 0`.
(a) Let `m` be an internal move on a colour `c ≠ k` from `v` to `v'` and `t` a `d = 1` term at `v`
not involving `c` (for (iii)_e: `e ≠ c`). The component from `v` to `w := t(m(…))` has exactly two
paths, `t` then `m` and `m` then the term `t'` of the same type at `v'`: a `d=1` term changes
colour `k` and at most one more colour, *upwards*, an internal move one colour *downwards*; the
net change of `c` is downward, so the internal move is on `c` and the gluing term has the same
type and extra colour. For `c ≡ 1` they cancel (Theorem A), so `c_t = c_{t'}`. Internal moves on
colours other than `k` (and `e`) connect all vertices with prescribed states of `k` (and `e`)
(each such colour toggles independently, in both envelopes), so `c^{(i)} ≡ λ_i`, `c^{(ii)} ≡ λ_{ii}`,
`c^{(iii)}_e ≡ λ_e`. (b) For `v` with `k ∈ B_L` and `w = N_{k−1,k}(v)` (`k → B_R`), the computation
of Theorem 5.1 (`D = 1`) shows exactly `n+1` paths, one per edge: `λ_k`·(i) (edge `k−1`),
(ii)·`μ_k` (edge `k`), and one through (iii)_e for each `e ≠ k` (edge `e−1` or `e`), with total
`Σ_x c_{(x)}(−1)^x q_x · (±1)`. Lemma 5.2 forces all `c_{(x)}` equal: `λ_i = λ_{ii} = λ_e =: λ`.
*Step 2 (d ≥ 2, induction downwards on `j`).* If `g_{l,k} = λZ_{l,k}` for `j < l < k`, subtracting
`λΦ_{j,k} = 0` from the `(j,k)` component gives `∂_jh + h∂_k = 0` for `h = g_{j,k} − λZ_{j,k}`
(a combination of the block-length-`D` terms, `D = k−j ≥ 2`). For a term `t` of `h` at `v` and a
colour `c ∈ [j+1,k−1]` (it is in `C` after `t`), the component `(v, w)`, `w` = (`t` then `μ_c`), has
only this path: the output block of a length-`D` term is `C^{D−1}B_R` or `C^D`, never with `B_R` at
`c < k`, which excludes "internal then `t'`"; and a different term followed by one internal move
cannot produce `w` ((ii) vs (i)/(iii) differ at `k`; (iii)_e vs (i), (iii)_{e'} differ at `e`). Its
contribution is `c'_t·(±q_c·monomial) ≠ 0` unless `c'_t = 0`. So `h = 0`. ∎
(`02_unique.py`: the full linear system, and Steps 1/2 separately, for `n ≤ 7` and all `k`:
solution spaces of dimension 1, resp. 1 and 0.)

### 7.4 Non-triviality without degree arguments

**Proposition 7.4.** For `1 ≤ k ≤ n`, the first-order component `G_{k−1,k}: S_k → S_{k−1}` (a chain
map up to sign) is not null-homotopic in the homotopy category of complexes of `A`-modules over `R`
(no restriction on the degree of a homotopy). Hence no gluing map `S_k → C_{k−1}` of the formula is
null-homotopic.

*Proof.* Apply the additive functor `F = e(s_k)·(−) = Pol_{b} ⊗_A −`, `s_k = e_{v_0(k)}`,
`b = o(s_k) = (0^k, 1^{n+1−k})` (edges `x < k` are 0). By the computation in Lemma 3.3, the internal move of an
L-colour `c ≤ k` flips edge `c−1` from 1 to `0 = b_{c−1}`, and that of an R-colour `c > k` flips edge
`c` from 0 to `1 = b_c`; both reach `b`, so the factors are `Q_{c−1}` resp. `Q_c` (Remark 3.4); so `F(S_k)` is the Koszul complex on `(Q_x)_{x ≠ k}`, and `F(S_{k−1})` the one on
`(Q_x)_{x ≠ k−1}`. Over `R` each such family is obtained from `(x_1, …, x_n)` by a triangular matrix
with unit diagonal (`Q_0, …, Q_{k−1}` in `x_1..x_k` with diagonal `u_1, t_{21}, …, t_{k,k−1}`;
`Q_n, …, Q_{k+1}` in `x_n..x_{k+1}` with diagonal `u_n, t_{n−1,n}, …`), so the homology is
`S/(x) = R`, at the base vertex. `G_{k−1,k}` maps `v_0(k)` only to `v_0(k−1)` (by (i)_1; (ii), (iii)
need `k ∈ B_L`), and the factor is `1` (the only flipped edge `k−1` goes `0 → 1 ≠ b_{k−1}`), so
`F(G_{k−1,k})` induces `±id_R` on homology. A null-homotopic map would induce 0. The composite of a
gluing map with the projection `C_{k−1} → S_{k−1}` is `G_{k−1,k}`. ∎

## 8. What the I-brane resolves (Theorem D)

**Theorem 8.1.** Over `R`, `H_i(E) = 0` for `i ≠ 0`, and `H_0(E) = L`, the `A`-module with
`e(s_B) L = R` (`s_B = W₁ 1 2 ⋯ n W_n`, dots acting by 0) and `e(s) L = 0` for every other state.
So the I-brane is a projective resolution of this one-dimensional (over `R`) simple module.

*Proof.* `e(s)` is exact and `e(s)E = K_{o(s)}` (Remark 3.4). For a given `b`, `K_b(S_k)` is a cube with
factor `Q_{c−1}` or `1` for each L-colour (`Q` iff `b_{c−1} = 0`) and `Q_c` or `1` for each R-colour
(`Q` iff `b_c = 1`); a factor `1` makes it contractible. So `K_b(S_k)` is non-contractible iff
`b_x = 0` for `x < k` and `b_x = 1` for `x > k`, and then `≃ R` (Prop. 7.4's argument). Filter `K_b`
by `k` (maps go down); the spectral sequence has `E_1 = ⊕_k H(K_b(S_k))`. If `b` is not of the form
`0^z1^{n+1−z}`, no `k` qualifies: `H = 0`. If `b = 0^z1^{n+1−z}` with `1 ≤ z ≤ n`, exactly `k ∈ {z−1, z}`
qualify, `b = b^{(z)}` and `d_1 = H(F(G_{z−1,z}))` is an isomorphism (Prop. 7.4): `E_2 = 0`. If
`b = 1^{n+1}`: only `k = 0`, `H = R` at `(0; ∅, ∅)`, `hdeg 0`. `b = 0^{n+1}` is impossible (it would
need `W_n` left of `W₁`). Finally `o(s) = 1^{n+1}` iff `s = s_B`. ∎
Check (`02_homology.py`, random specialisation mod `p`): for every state, exactness at a random
point `x`, and at `x = 0` homology `binom(n, i)` in degree `i` for `s_B` (Tor of the residue field) and
0 otherwise: all states for `n ≤ 4`, 60 states for `n = 5`.

(Over `R_pol` the `Q`'s are not related to the `x`'s by an invertible matrix, and the homology is
different; not computed.)
